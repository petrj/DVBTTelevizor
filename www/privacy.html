DVBT Televizor Agreement

Purpose of the Application

DVBT Televizor is an application for Android devices—including smartphones, tablets, and Android TVs—that enables users to watch DVB-T and DVB-T2 digital television broadcasts using a compatible USB DVB-T/DVB-T2/RTL SDR tuner. In addition to television, the application also supports listening to FM radio and DAB/DAB+ digital radio, providing a complete mobile TV and radio experience.
